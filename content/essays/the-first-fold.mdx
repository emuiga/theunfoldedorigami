# The First Fold

Every idea begins as a single fold—a crease in the fabric of thought. This is where we start: not with answers, but with questions. Not with clarity, but with curiosity.

## The Beginning

Origami teaches us patience. A single sheet of paper, through careful folding, transforms into something entirely new. Ideas work the same way. We start with a simple thought, a raw observation, a moment of wonder.

## The Process

Each fold requires intention. There is no rushing, no skipping steps. Each crease builds upon the last, creating structure from chaos, meaning from confusion.

In writing, as in origami, the process matters more than the destination. We don't know what shape our thoughts will take until we commit to the work of unfolding them.

## The Unfolding

Gradually, what was once hidden becomes visible. What was once folded becomes clear. This is the magic of both paper and prose: transformation through deliberate action.

We don't just reveal ideas—we create them, one fold at a time.
